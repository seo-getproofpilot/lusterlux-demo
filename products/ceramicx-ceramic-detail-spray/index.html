CeramicX — Ceramic Spray

Bonded protection, one panel at a time.

CeramicX bonds to the surface and leaves a durable, hydrophobic layer that repels water, road grime and UV. Gloss goes up, dirt has less to grab, and the next wash takes half as long. It layers on top of a coating you already have rather than replacing it, so it works as both a standalone protectant and a topper between full ceramic services.

How to use

  1. Start with a clean, dry, cool surface.
  2. Mist two to three sprays per panel.
  3. Spread evenly with an Edgeless Lux towel.
  4. Flip to a dry towel and buff until it disappears.
  5. Give it an hour before it sees rain.
