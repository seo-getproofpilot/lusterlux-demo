What it does

  • Short body You are not fighting a two-foot wand around a wheel arch.
  • Full quick-connect nozzle set 0°, 15°, 25°, 40° and soap, so one gun covers every job.
  • One-click swap to the cannon Foam and rinse without changing setup.
  • Built to last Metal fittings rather than moulded plastic.
Never use the 0° nozzle on paintIt is for concrete and wheel wells. On a panel it will damage the finish.

Detailer tips

  • 40° for rinsing paint, 25° for wheel wells, 15° for stubborn ground-in dirt.
  • Keep a foot of distance from any panel.
  • Rinse top to bottom, always.
  • Drain it after use if it lives somewhere that freezes.
