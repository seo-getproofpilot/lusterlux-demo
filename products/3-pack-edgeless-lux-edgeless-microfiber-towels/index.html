Edgeless Lux — Exterior Microfiber, 3-Pack

The towel the formulas were built around.

A stitched edge is a hard seam being pulled across your clear coat. These are edgeless, high-pile and exterior weight, which is why every waterless and ceramic instruction on this site says to use them.

What it does

  • No stitched edge A sewn border is a hard seam being dragged across your clear coat. These have none.
  • Exterior weight High pile, so grit lifts up into the nap instead of staying pinned against the paint.
  • What the formulas were built around Every waterless and ceramic instruction on this site says to use these.
  • Three to a pack A full vehicle takes four to six towel faces. Fold in quarters and you get eight per towel.

Detailer tips

  • Keep exterior and interior towels in separate piles.
  • Wash warm, no fabric softener — it coats the fibres and kills absorbency.
  • The moment one touches the ground it becomes a wheel towel for life.
  • If a towel comes out slick rather than grabby, it is contaminated.

Best for

  • Waterless washing
  • Ceramic application
  • Final buffing
