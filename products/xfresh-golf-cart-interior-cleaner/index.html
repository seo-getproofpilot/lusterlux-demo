XFresh — Cart Interior Cleaner

Seats you can actually sit on.

Cart seats live outdoors and take sunscreen, sweat and grass all season. XFresh cleans the vinyl, dash and trim and dries clean — no slick residue, which matters a lot more on a bench seat with no belts.

What it does

  • Dries clean on vinyl No slick film, which matters a lot on a bench seat with no belts.
  • Made for cart interiors Seats, dash, trim and the textured vinyl carts are covered in.
  • Handles a season outdoors Sunscreen, sweat, grass and rain, which is what cart seats actually take.
  • No greasy residue Nothing transfers onto clothes.

How to use

  1. Spray onto a towel, not the seat.
  2. Work one section at a time.
  3. Wipe and follow with a dry side.
  4. Use a LuxBrush on textured vinyl.

Detailer tips

  • Spray onto the towel, not the seat.
  • Work one section and follow with a dry towel face.
  • A LuxBrush gets into textured vinyl a towel skates over.
  • Do the seats before the bodywork so overspray lands on unwashed panels.
