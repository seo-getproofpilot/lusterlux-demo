TireVenom — Tire Dressing

Deep black that stays on the tire.

Most tire shine is silicone that never really dries, which is why it ends up in a stripe along your rocker panel. TireVenom dries to the touch and stays where you put it. It restores faded rubber to a deep, even finish and shields it against UV, cracking and browning, without the greasy film that attracts dust the moment you park.

What it does

  • No-sling, dries to the touch Stays on the sidewall instead of striping down your rocker panel on the first pull-out.
  • Deep, even black Restores faded rubber without the wet-look gloss that reads cheap.
  • UV, cracking and browning defence Up to three months from a single application.
  • Never greasy It does not stay tacky, so it does not collect dust the moment you park.
  • 30 to 50 tires a bottle A 16 oz bottle lasts most people a year.
Scrub the sidewall clean firstDressing over old dressing is exactly what causes browning. This step is not optional.

How to use

  1. Scrub the sidewall clean first — dressing over old dressing is what causes browning.
  2. Let the tire dry completely.
  3. Apply an even coat with an XPad applicator.
  4. Level it out so there are no heavy spots.
  5. Let it set before you drive.

Detailer tips

  • Apply with an XPad rather than spraying directly.
  • Level out any heavy spots before it sets.
  • Let it set before you drive.
  • Dress tires last, after the car is washed and dry.
